What changed in the implementation

  • Bound promotion admission allocations and per-block switch destinations before CFG construction.
  • Combine call/load-reuse admission scans and skip callers with fewer than two ordinary reads.
  • Lazily cache running-state CFG, invalidate after every successful conversion.
  • Remove ARM dispatch experiment from normal builds; require experimental-dispatch.

experimental-dispatch is now required by callers of ExperimentalDispatchTails. The default Full schedule remains 62 passes; Fast is unchanged.

What caused the concern?

  • Transport is not free. Scalar arguments can remove loads while adding moves and register pressure. Some individual QuickJS functions grew substantially.
  • Cleanup alone was insufficient. The ablation identified transport plus cleanup as the useful combination; call/load reuse added no clear application benefit.
  • Stop changing inactive modules. The new gate skips all extra cleanup when no signature changes. Lua and LZ4 ARM executables are byte-identical to baseline, eliminating a code-change explanation for their timing variation.

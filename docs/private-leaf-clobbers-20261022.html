Where it belongs

1 · Optimize IRInline, fold and simplify first. Full now classifies private calls after its final cleanup; Fast keeps its existing schedule.
2 · Start code generationSelect, allocate and emit eligible leaves into a bounded cache. Record registers used by their finalized allocations and transfers.
3 · Allocate callersIntersect preservation proofs across every call a value crosses. Unknown calls restore ABI restrictions.
4 · Emit the moduleReuse the exact cached leaf bodies and retain original function order.

This is already the implemented placement. Moving it to the start of the IR pipeline would precede the physical-register decisions it summarizes. Existing IR passes have no extra optimization triggered by the Fast tag; earlier memory-effect or aggregate summaries would be separate work.

How could it regress?

More register choices can change a greedy allocator’s later eviction decisions. Different assignments also change dependencies and instruction layout. Those tradeoffs can affect cycles even when total instructions fall.

The yyjson slowdown in the seven-round screen did not survive the follow-up as a clear regression. Its 31-round interval includes parity. CPU scheduling and frequency variation also affect small measurements.
